🔐 Pain World — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Pain World is an independent third-party Discord application providing moderation, server management, security, AutoMod, AI assistance, music, ticket, greeting, utility, and entertainment features.

1. Information Pain World May Access

Depending on enabled features and Discord permissions, Pain World may process information available through Discord’s API, including Discord user IDs, guild/server IDs, usernames and display names, avatars, roles, channel IDs, role IDs, message content when required for a feature, message metadata, voice-channel information when required for music features, and commands or interactions sent to the Bot.

2. Information We Store

Depending on enabled features, Pain World may store configuration such as server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, no-prefix user IDs, greeting settings, ticket settings, AutoMod settings, security settings, and other server-specific settings.

Ticket functionality may generate transcripts when a server has configured transcript storage.

3. Command and Feature Data

When you interact directly with Pain World, the Bot may process information contained in your command or interaction to perform the requested feature, including moderation, tickets, translation, AI, music, utility, greeting, and entertainment functions.

4. AI Features

When AI features are used, text submitted to the Bot may be sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a response. Third-party providers may process submitted information under their own policies. Avoid sending passwords, API keys, financial information, or other confidential information to AI features.

5. Translation Features

Translation requests may be sent to third-party translation services in order to provide a translation. Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information for translation.

6. Music Features

Music functionality may interact with third-party music, media, search, or metadata services. Search queries, track information, or related metadata may be processed by those services when music commands are used.

7. Tickets and Transcripts

If ticket transcripts are enabled, messages inside a ticket may be included in a transcript. A transcript can contain usernames, user IDs, message content, timestamps, and attachment URLs. Server administrators are responsible for configuring access to transcript channels appropriately.

8. How We Use Information

  • Operate Pain World
  • Execute commands and interactions
  • Provide moderation and management features
  • Provide security and AutoMod features
  • Store server configuration
  • Provide ticket and transcript functionality
  • Provide greeting and automation features
  • Provide AI responses and translations
  • Provide music, utility, and entertainment features
  • Prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems
  • Maintain and improve the Bot

9. Data Sharing

Necessary information may be transmitted to third-party service providers used by Pain World, which may include AI providers, translation providers, music/media providers, and hosting or infrastructure providers. We do not sell Discord user information.

10. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary for security or legitimate operational purposes. Server configuration may remain stored until it is removed or reset. Third-party providers may apply their own retention periods.
